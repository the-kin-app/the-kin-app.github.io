Min gets people out of the house to spend time together somewhere real. It matches people who are nearby and up for company, then sends them to one specific venue to actually be there — two people meeting, or a whole group. Sometimes decided ten minutes beforehand, sometimes planned days ahead. Either way people arrive at a place with a reason to be in it, and that venue would pay only when they turn up. No advertising, nothing to post, nothing for your staff to do.
