Step 3 of 4

The thing we're considering

We're building an app that gets people out of the house to meet face to face. It matches people who are nearby and up for company right now, and points them at a spot on the street just outside a venue — sometimes two of them, sometimes a few. Ten minutes into standing around talking, going inside is the natural next move, and a venue would pay only when they actually do. No advertising, nothing to post, nothing for your staff to do.
