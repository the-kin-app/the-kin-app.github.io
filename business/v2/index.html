Question 5 of 10

Here's the idea. What do you think?

min matches people nearby who are up for meeting someone new, and sends them to one specific place to do it. Two people meeting for a chat, a small group doing an activity, or friend groups meeting each other. Your venue could be that place, and you'd pay only for guests who actually walk in. No ads, nothing to post, nothing extra for your staff.
