built to get you off your phone

Screen-time limits make the phone harder. min makes real life easier.

  • One tap, not six steps

    Deciding what to do, finding company, agreeing on a time and place, paying. Usually that's three apps and a group chat, and the free hour is gone. With min, every offer comes with all of it already sorted.

  • Pay once, then just go

    The coffee, the lunch set, the museum ticket: you pay for it in the app when you join. Nothing to book, split or sort out at the counter when you get there.

  • No feed, no swiping, no chat

    We want you out there, not scrolling to watch other people do it. min makes money when you go do something, not from your screen time. The app is meant to be boring to hold.

Questions people ask

If we've missed yours, reply to the waitlist email — a person answers it.

Is min a dating app?

No. min finds you something to do nearby, alone or with people who share an interest. The activity is the point. What happens after it is up to you.

Do I have to go with strangers?

No. Go alone, bring a friend, or join people nearby who are up for the same thing. Solo activities skip the wait entirely.

How is min different from Meetup or event apps?

Events are planned days or weeks ahead. min is for the free hour you have right now: live offers close by that you can go to straight away, with the time, place and payment already sorted in one tap.

Does it cost money?

Using min is free, with no subscription. When you join an activity you prepay its item, like the coffee or the museum ticket, plus a small service fee. You see the full price before you accept, and if a group doesn't fill in time you get your money back.

What does min know about me?

What you tell it in short chats and which activities you go to, so the offers fit you better. You can see, correct or delete it at any time. We don't sell your data, and min is built for GDPR from day one.

Is it safe?

Activities happen in public venues, never anyone's home. min is 18+, with your date of birth confirmed at signup. Others only see a nickname, avatar, short intro and age range. You can cancel up to the meeting with a neutral “plans changed”, and block or report in one tap. Every report is reviewed by a person within 24 hours.
