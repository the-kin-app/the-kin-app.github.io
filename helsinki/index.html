how it works

Three steps from your phone to a hello

  1. 1
    min gets to know you

    min is curious and wants to talk to you. No forms, no photos: a short conversation is how min learns who you are and who you'd like to meet.

  2. 2
    min finds your people nearby

    min looks for people around you who want the same thing you do: on your campus, in your neighbourhood, at the café down the street.

  3. 3
    Go say hello

    Accept the intro and the rest is up to you. Walk over and say hello, somewhere public.

not like all the other “social” apps

Helsinki is full of people you pass every day and never talk to. min is here to change that.

  • Image
    No swiping or chatting

    This isn't a dating app or a friend-making app. It just makes saying hello to the people around you normal again. Once you're matched there is one thing left to do: walk over and say it. No match to unmatch, and no chat left hanging if it goes nowhere.

  • Image
    No profiles

    No profile picture, no bio to keep updated. Min simply learns about you and keeps that memory on your device. Nobody is sorted by their face, and there is no profile for us to sell, because there is no profile.

  • Image
    No feed

    We want you out there in the city, not scrolling to watch other people do it. We make money when you get off your phone, not by maximising your screen time. The app is meant to be boring to hold.

Is min a dating app?

No. We usually say that min is a "social lubricant" app, meaning its only goal is to make saying hello to people around you easier. What the relationship becomes after that, a friend, a running group, or a date, is up to the two of you.

How is min different from Meetup, Bumble BFF or other friend apps?

There is no profile to build, nothing to swipe and no group chat to keep alive. You talk to min, min introduces you to one person nearby, and if you both accept, you walk over and say hello.

Can people find me on this? Is it safe?

There's no search, no profiles to browse and no followers. min acts as the intermediary that maintains each user's anonymity until an introduction is mutually accepted. The meeting only happens in a public, populated area, and there is always a smooth option to back out of the meeting at any time.

What does min actually store about me?

The conversation you have with min is used by min to tune your memory shape, then deleted. Your memory shape is only stored on your phone. During matching, it sends an anonymized version of your memory to find the right person, then is disposed. We don't sell your private data because we literally don't have access to it.

Does it cost money?

The basic use of talking to min and getting introductions from min are free forever. We are planning a subscription to give users more control over when and who they get introduced to, as well as curated group events, but the price is not yet determined.
