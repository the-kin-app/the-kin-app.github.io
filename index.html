Most of what a social app does, Min doesn't

Not to be contrary. Each of these is something we found actively gets in the way of two people meeting.

  • No profile pictures

    Nobody is sorted by their face. Min introduces you with a name and a reason, and you find out what someone looks like the same way you always have — by turning up.

  • No swiping, no feed

    There is nothing to scroll. Min brings you one introduction at a time, and when there isn't one, the app has nothing to show you. It is meant to be boring to hold.

  • Your memory stays on your phone

    What Min learns about you lives on your device. When it looks for someone nearby it sends only what that one introduction needs — and never the conversation behind it.

  • No pressure to be friends or date

    An introduction is one hello, not a relationship you now owe someone. There's no match to unmatch and no chat left hanging if it goes nowhere.

  • Only what you can walk to

    Min knows a few hundred metres around you and nothing else. No strangers from the other side of the planet, because you can't go and meet them.

  • No streaks

    Min is capped on how often it can reach you. There is nothing to keep alive and nothing lost by ignoring it for a week.

Questions people ask

If we've missed yours, reply to the waitlist email — a person answers it.

Is Min a dating app?

No. Min introduces you to people worth meeting and doesn't decide what that turns into. Some of it is a coffee, some of it is a walk with someone you'd never have spoken to, some of it is nothing. That's yours to work out, not the app's.

Do I have to make a profile?

No. Talking to Min is the profile. No photo, no bio, no prompts to answer, nothing that goes stale the moment you stop maintaining it.

What does Min actually know about me?

Whatever you've told it, held on your phone. To find you someone nearby it sends the minimum that introduction needs, and you see what that is before anyone else does. We don't sell data — not as a policy you have to trust us on, but because nothing in the system is built to move it.

Will it keep pinging me?

Min has a hard cap on how often it's allowed to contact you. A nudge when something is genuinely worth leaving the house for, and silence the rest of the time. There are no streaks to keep and nothing to come back to daily.

Can someone look me up on it?

There's no search, no profiles to browse and no followers. An introduction only becomes real when both people say yes to it, and until then neither of you is findable by the other.

What will it cost?

We haven't set pricing yet, and we'd rather say that than invent a number. What we have ruled out is advertising and selling data — both require holding your attention, which is the opposite of what this is for.

When does it reach my city?

Helsinki first, one neighbourhood at a time. A hyperlocal app is useless until the people near you are on it, so we'd rather be dense in a few streets than thin across a continent. Join the waitlist and we'll go where people are waiting.
