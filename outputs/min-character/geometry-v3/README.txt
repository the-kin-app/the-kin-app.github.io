MIN — IDLE GEOMETRY V3

Main file: Min_Idle_Review.blend
The previous model remains in the parent directory.

Rebuilt broad domed/squircle body, small integrated side flaps, two rounded continuous feet, and single-sided surface eye patches. No eye sockets or solid eye lenses.

Body base mesh: 4,172 vertices / 4,170 quads. Closed manifold surface; exact bilateral symmetry. Non-destructive subdivision and UVs included.
Runtime GLB: three meshes, 34,416 triangles, one subdivision level baked. USDZ also provided. Both exports successfully re-imported into Blender. Native web/iOS performance and rendering are not yet tested.

The neutral clay material is for silhouette review. Satin preview is a second surface check. Final glow material is deferred.
Skeleton, weights, expression shape keys, and animation clips are not included in this geometry revision. These are the next production steps; topology should be deformation-tested when rigging. Eye patches need corresponding deformation or a surface-mask implementation for expressions.

Reference: Figma pages 03 Min and 06 Min chat; front idle prioritized. Corrupted extra feet in generated side references ignored. Outdated Min material spec removed from page 06 as authorized.
