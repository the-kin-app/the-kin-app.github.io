Privacy Policy

Who we are

"Kin" ("we", "us") is the project behind the Kin app, currently in pre-launch development. Kin doesn't yet have a separate registered legal entity — the contact below reaches the person building it directly.

Contact for anything in this policy: onni.nikku@proton.me

What we collect

  • A name or nickname — whatever you're comfortable giving us, no legal name required
  • Either an email address or a phone number, whichever you choose to provide when joining the waitlist

We only ask for one contact method, not both, so we don't hold more than we need.

Why we collect it

We use your details to:

  • Notify you when Kin launches
  • Send occasional updates about Kin's development before launch (e.g. progress updates, beta invites)

We don't use your details for anything else, and we don't send unrelated marketing. Our legal basis for this processing is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given when you submit the waitlist form. You can withdraw consent at any time — see "Your rights" below.

Where your data is stored

This site is currently a test deployment. As published, the waitlist form does not transmit or store any data anywhere.

Once the waitlist is actually live, submissions will be stored in a database we control directly (for example, Supabase/Postgres). We don't sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We'll update this section with the specific provider before real signups are collected.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist entries until three months after Kin launches, after which they're deleted. If you ask us to delete your data sooner, we will.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over the data we hold about you, including the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this won't affect processing that already happened)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these, email onni.nikku@proton.me — for a list this small, we handle requests directly rather than through an automated process.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

Kin's waitlist isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the project develops — for example, once a real backend or a registered company entity is in place. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
