Privacy Policy

Who we are

"Min" ("we", "us") is the project behind the Min app, currently in pre-launch development. Min doesn't yet have a separate registered legal entity — the contact below reaches the person building it directly.

Contact for anything in this policy: hello@hellomin.app

What we collect

Waitlist (hellomin.app/waitlist/):

  • A name or nickname — whatever you're comfortable giving us, no legal name required
  • Either an email address or a phone number, whichever you choose to provide when joining the waitlist

We only ask for one contact method, not both, so we don't hold more than we need.

Business interest form (hellomin.app/business/):

  • Business name and location — the only two required fields
  • Optionally: business type, your name, an email or phone number, and your answers to a short questionnaire about how you currently market your business, slow periods, your reaction to a hyperlocal-offers concept we're researching, pricing preferences, and interest in a pilot

Every field on the business form beyond business name and location is optional — you choose how much to share. We do not collect your IP address, browser/device information, or any other technical metadata when you submit either form.

Why we collect it

We use waitlist details to:

  • Notify you when Min launches
  • Send occasional updates about Min's development before launch (e.g. progress updates, beta invites)

We use business form details to:

  • Understand whether a hyperlocal offers/ads feature would be worth building, and how to price and design it
  • Contact you about a pilot, if you've asked to hear about one and we run one in your area

We don't use your details for anything else, and we don't send unrelated marketing. Our legal basis for this processing is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given when you submit either form. You can withdraw consent at any time — see "Your rights" below.

Where your data is stored

Submissions from both forms are stored in a database we control directly, hosted on Cloudflare (D1, Cloudflare's serverless database product), reachable only through a small API we wrote ourselves. Cloudflare acts as our infrastructure provider/processor for this data — it doesn't use submissions for its own purposes, and data may be processed on Cloudflare's infrastructure outside your country, under Cloudflare's standard contractual safeguards for international transfers.

We don't sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist entries until three months after Min launches, after which they're deleted. Business form submissions are kept while we're actively researching and building this feature, and deleted once we've either shelved the idea or moved past the research stage. If you ask us to delete your data sooner, we will.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over the data we hold about you, including the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this won't affect processing that already happened)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these, email hello@hellomin.app — for a list this small, we handle requests directly rather than through an automated process.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

Neither Min's waitlist nor its business form is directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the project develops — for example, once a real backend or a registered company entity is in place. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
