Here's what Min does.

This part is the free version, and it stays free.

You talk to Min for a few minutes. From that conversation it works out what you're like and what you're up for, and it never asks you to fill in a profile. Then, when someone nearby is a good fit and you're both up for it, Min introduces the two of you and suggests a public spot to meet, right then. No browsing, no profiles, no followers.

And anyone can put up something to do later the same day — a coffee, a run, a board game — for other people nearby to join.
